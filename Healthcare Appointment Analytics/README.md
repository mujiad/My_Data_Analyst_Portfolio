### Healthcare Appointment Data Insights
## Patient & Demographic Insights
## Average Patient Age
- The average patient age is 54 years old.
## Most Common Age Group
- The Senior age group (56 years old and above) has the highest number of clinic visits.
- This is followed by:
    - Young Adults (16 to 35)
    - Adults (36 to 55)
## Age Group with the Highest Billing
- Seniors generated the highest total billing amount.
- Young Adults ranked second.
- Adults generated the lowest total billing among the three groups.
## Relationship Between Age and Billing
- Seniors contributed the highest overall billing and appointment counts, especially in the Neurology and Orthopedics departments.
- Neurology and Orthopedics consistently showed:
    - High appointment volumes
    - High average billing amounts
    - Strong contribution to total revenue across all age groups
- Some departments generated higher revenue despite having fewer appointments because their average billing per appointment was higher. This may indicate:
    - More complex treatments
    - Higher-cost procedures
    - Specialized services

### Appointment & Scheduling Insights
## Year with the Highest Appointments
- 2025 recorded the highest total number of appointments.
- However, 2026 recorded the highest average monthly appointments despite only containing data for three months.
## Appointment Trend Over Time
- Appointments generally increased over time.
- In 2025:
    - The year started with low appointment counts
    - Appointments gradually increased throughout the year
    - Average monthly appointments reached approximately 76 patients
- In 2026:
    - The year started with already high appointment counts
    - Average monthly appointments increased further to approximately 80 patients
    - This may indicate a growing demand for clinic services and suggests that 2026 could potentially become the busiest year if the trend continues.
    - The clinic may consider preparing additional resources, staff, and scheduling capacity to accommodate possible increases in patient volume.
## Busiest Month
- October 2025 was the busiest month with 104 patient appointments.
- The second busiest month was November 2025 and February 2026 having 91 appointment each.
## Lowest Booking Period
- March 2025 which is the starting month of the appointment record shows the lowest number of appointments with only 15 patients.
## Most Visited Department
- The departments ranked by appointment volume are:
1. Neurology
2. Orthopedics
3. Cardiology
4. General Medicine

### Revenue & Financial Insights
## Total Revenue
- The clinic generated a total revenue of 211.19K USD.
## Highest Earning Department
- Neurology generated the highest total revenue.
- This was followed by:
    - Orthopedics
    - Cardiology
    - General Medicine
## Revenue Drivers
- Neurology generated the highest revenue primarily because it had the highest number of patient appointments.
- Orthopedics also contributed significant revenue due to:
    - High appointment counts
    - Higher average billing per appointment compared to some departments
## Currency Usage
- The most commonly used billing currency is:
1. INR
2. USD
3. EUR
4. GBP

### Key Overall Findings
- Seniors are the clinic’s primary patient group and also contribute the largest share of billing revenue.
- Neurology is the clinic’s strongest-performing department in both appointment volume and revenue generation.
- Appointment activity shows an increasing trend over time, indicating growing patient demand.
- Some departments with fewer appointments still generate substantial revenue because of higher average treatment costs.
- INR is the dominant billing currency, suggesting that a large portion of patients or transactions are associated with India-based billing.
